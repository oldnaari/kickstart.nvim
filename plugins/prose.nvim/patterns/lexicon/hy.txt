# Armenian word lists for prose.nvim's pattern flags (patterns/prose_patterns.py).
#
# A starting point only: no published style lists like the English and Russian
# ones exist for Armenian, so these are short and meant to grow. Add what you
# notice in your own writing.
#
# One entry per line under a [section]; [weak-verb] and [acronym-ok] take
# several words to a line. Words match as written or by their
# dictionary form (verbs in -ել/-ալ), so "հանդիսանալ" finds "հանդիսանում է".
# Punctuation is ignored; ։ and : end sentences. A word may carry a part of
# speech: "շատ/ADV" matches only the adverb, not "շատ" as "many".
# Edits apply from the next paragraph parsed.
